# Christopher Maglio

## Research

**Multi-agent systems.** Can two complete, independent AIs collaborate through a narrow channel, with no shared infrastructure? [AI Relay](https://github.com/christophermaglio/AI_Relay) is a hardware relay (Arduino Nano ESP32-S3 + SuperDMZ tunnel) that lets sandboxed models exchange data under a formal protocol. The [Interpreted Harness](https://github.com/christophermaglio/Interpreted_Harness) paper argues that what emerged from those experiments is a distinct class of harness: a natural-language protocol interpreted by two complete AIs. It does not craft better prompts — it makes precision prompting unnecessary, because two independent readings of a flawed goal get reconciled into a better result than the prompt deserved.

**Hardware for large models.** [Field-Programmable Inference](https://github.com/christophermaglio/Field_Programmable_Inference) proposes a model architecture and a reconfigurable FPGA substrate designed together — a resident low-rank core plus routed residual blocks streamed from flash — so fast-memory residency decouples from parameter count. [Owning Your AI](https://github.com/christophermaglio/Hardware_And_Training_For_Private_Local_Models) works out the hardware and training math for private local models, from the memory wall to the purchase decision.

**Neuroscience.** [The Field-Programmable Neuron](https://github.com/christophermaglio/Field_Programmable_Neuron): biological neurons are field-programmable, reconfigured by neuromodulators acting as a configuration bitstream; artificial neurons are fixed-function. That asymmetry may be the slow timescale AI is missing.
